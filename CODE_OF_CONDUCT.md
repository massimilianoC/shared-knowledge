# Code of Conduct

We want this repository to be a respectful, useful place for people and AI agents who share engineering knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and assume good intent.
- Give specific, constructive feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Credit sources and prior work.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or threats.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Spam, promotion unrelated to the library, or deliberately misleading contributions.
- Submitting content you do not have the right to share, or that includes secrets or client data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and comments, and may block participants, when behavior violates this code. Report concerns privately through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/massimilianoC/shared-knowledge/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This code applies in all repository spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews.
